PhotoStereo XR

Your photos in 3D on Meta Quest

Look around your photos, not just at them. PhotoStereo XR uses on-device AI to turn the photos on your Meta Quest into stereoscopic 3D in seconds.

Volume mode

Your photo gains real relief. Lean in or move your head and the image shifts like a real object in front of you.

Cinema 3D mode

A flat screen with stereoscopic depth. Comfortable, no need to move.

Private and unlimited

  • Everything runs on the headset: no uploads, no accounts, works offline.
  • No credits or subscriptions: convert as many photos as you want.
  • Conversion queue, a "3D photos" album, and side-by-side 3D copies saved to the Meta Quest gallery.
  • Interface in English, Portuguese and Spanish.

Support

Questions, feedback or bug reports: photostereoxr.app@gmail.com

Tips for the best results: use sharp, high-resolution photos of people, faces and objects with a clear subject. To get photos onto your headset, copy them to the Pictures folder over USB, or use the photos and screenshots already on your Meta Quest.
