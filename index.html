Tips and support

Get the best 3D

Best photos

  • Sharp, high-resolution photos with a clear main subject.
  • People, pets and objects in front of a deeper background look amazing in Volume.
  • Very dark photos give less depth.

Getting photos onto the Quest

  • Connect the Quest via USB and copy photos to the Pictures folder.
  • Or use photos and screenshots already on the headset.
